gold.mart_market  (16 columns)
    - symbol: Trading pair, for example BTCUSDT.
    - window_start: Start of the hourly window in UTC.
    - open: First one-minute open in the hour.
    - high: Highest one-minute high in the hour.
    - low: Lowest one-minute low in the hour.
    - close: Last one-minute close in the hour.
    - volume: Base-asset volume traded in the hour.
    - quote_volume: Quote-asset volume traded in the hour.
    - trade_count: Number of trades in the hour.
    - vwap: Volume-weighted average price for the hour.
    - log_return_1h: Natural log of close over the previous hour close.
    - realized_vol_1h: Standard deviation of one-minute log returns inside the hour.
    - rv_ewma_24h: Exponentially weighted volatility with a 24-hour span.
    - source: binance_ws, binance_vision, or mixed when the hour has both.
    - updated_at: Materialization timestamp; excluded from idempotence hashes.
    - event_date: Iceberg partition column, derived from window_start.

gold.mart_ml  (23 columns)
    - symbol: Trading pair.
    - window_start: Feature window boundary and label window start (UTC).
    - ret_5m: Log return over the trailing 5 minutes.
    - ret_15m: Log return over the trailing 15 minutes.
    - ret_60m: Log return over the trailing 60 minutes.
    - rv_5m: Realized volatility over the trailing 5 minutes.
    - rv_15m: Realized volatility over the trailing 15 minutes.
    - rv_60m: Realized volatility over the trailing 60 minutes.
    - volume_z_60m: Volume z-score against the trailing 60 minutes.
    - trade_count_60m: Trades in the trailing 60 minutes.
    - hl_range_1m: High-low range of the last minute, divided by close.
    - vwap_dev: Close deviation from the trailing 60-minute VWAP.
    - sent_mean_1h: Mean sentiment of posts in the previous hour.
    - sent_pos_share: Positive share of posts in the previous hour.
    - sent_neg_share: Negative share of posts in the previous hour.
    - post_count_1h: Post volume in the previous hour.
    - engagement_1h: Engagement per post in the previous hour.
    - label_rv_next_1h: Realized volatility over (window_start, window_start + 1h].
    - label_start: Label window start, always equal to window_start.
    - label_end: Label window end, always window_start + 1 hour.
    - feature_snapshot_id: Iceberg snapshot or build id the features came from.
    - dq_status: pass when every feature and the label are present, else warn.
    - event_date: Iceberg partition column, derived from window_start.

gold.mart_sentiment  (15 columns)
    - window_start: Start of the hourly window in UTC.
    - post_count: Unique posts created in the hour; 0 for an empty hour.
    - unique_authors: Distinct pseudonymized authors in the hour.
    - mean_sentiment: Mean VADER compound score in [-1, 1]; null when empty.
    - positive_share: Share of posts labelled positive; null when empty.
    - negative_share: Share of posts labelled negative; null when empty.
    - neutral_share: Share of posts labelled neutral; null when empty.
    - total_likes: Sum of likes across the hour's posts.
    - total_reposts: Sum of reposts across the hour's posts.
    - total_replies: Sum of replies across the hour's posts.
    - engagement_per_post: Likes plus reposts plus replies, divided by post_count.
    - top_hashtags: Up to five hashtags ordered by frequency.
    - sentiment_model_version: Lexicon or model version that produced the scores.
    - updated_at: Materialization timestamp; excluded from idempotence hashes.
    - event_date: Iceberg partition column, derived from window_start.
